## 🔎 Key Business Insights

**📈 Revenue Trends**

January recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $6.8M, while February and October showed revenue dips.

**💊 Product Performance**

Doxycycline was the leading product by revenue and profit. Lisinopril recorded the highest sales volume at approximately 11.1K units.

**🌎 Regional Performance**

Canada generated approximately $31M in revenue, followed by Australia at approximately $15M.

**👥 Customer Value**

Customer 191 generated approximately $803,622 in revenue, highlighting an opportunity to examine high-value customer purchasing patterns.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Investigate the drivers behind January's revenue peak and the dips in February and October.
- Evaluate the factors contributing to Doxycycline's strong revenue and profitability.
- Explore opportunities to strengthen performance across regional markets.
- Analyze high-value customer purchasing patterns to support customer retention and sales planning.
